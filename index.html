    Founder & CEO

    I Love Product People

    Experienced product operators, paired with early-stage founders, paid in equity.

    Early-stage founders make product decisions alone. Senior product people have the scars to help, but their expertise gets reduced to an hourly rate or a favor. I Love Product People pairs the two for a 4 to 6 week engagement scoped to one outcome, compensated in equity. Strong fits can become long-term partners or co-founders. Curated, not a marketplace.

    • Since Aug 2026: 36 outreach messages, 5 validation calls across Germany, Spain, and the US.
    • Co-founded with Sylvester Mobley.
